About

Reliable AI, from mechanism to embodiment

I am a Ph.D. student at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences, in the State Key Laboratory of Multimodal Artificial Intelligence Systems, advised by Prof. Xu-Yao Zhang.

My research asks why large multimodal models and agents fail, and how to make them reliable. I open up their internal computation (information flow, attention, and positional structure) to find where hallucination, repetition, and spatial confusion begin. I then turn these mechanistic insights into principled fixes at both training and inference time. I carry the same reliability goals into self-evolving agents and embodied systems, from 3D spatial perception to vision–language–action (VLA) and world-action models.
